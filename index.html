不该停的时候绝不打扰。

Rest on time.
Never at the wrong moment.

FocusRest Pro 让计时器读懂你正在做的事。

FocusRest Pro teaches the timer to read the room.

  • 视频会议中 · 摄像头或麦克风被占用时自动暂停
  • 屏幕录制 / 共享中 · 遮罩绝不抢屏
  • 长时间离开 · 空闲自动暂停，回来自动继续
  • 休眠与锁屏 · 计时智能挂起，不误算你的休息
  • 到点想再战一会儿？延迟几分钟，或跳过这次休息
  • On a call · pauses the moment the camera or mic is in use
  • Screen recording · the overlay never steals your frame
  • Away from desk · idle auto-pause, resumes when you return
  • Sleep & lock · the timer suspends itself, never miscounted
  • Five more minutes — literally: delay a break, or skip it

简约，是认真的克制

Simplicity, taken seriously

菜单栏、设置、统计，每一屏都只留下必要的东西。设置页预览与真实休息页共用同一套排版，所见即所得。

Menu bar, settings, stats — every screen keeps only what's necessary. Settings previews render the real break page itself, so what you see is exactly what you get.

你的休息数据，只属于你

Your rest data belongs to you alone

FocusRest 不收集、不上传、不共享任何个人数据。没有账户体系，没有分析 SDK，沙盒里甚至没有网络权限。统计留在你的 Mac 上，随时可以导出，卸载即清空。

FocusRest collects, uploads and shares nothing. No accounts, no analytics SDKs — the sandbox doesn't even grant network access. Statistics stay on your Mac, exportable anytime, deleted when you uninstall.
